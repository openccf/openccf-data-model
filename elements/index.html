    OpenCCF - Open Corporate Carbon Footprint Data Model

    An open, interoperable data model for exchanging GHG Protocol-aligned corporate carbon footprints between systems. Released under CC0-1.0.

    URI: https://w3id.org/openccf

    Name: openccf
